Rules

The short version

Organizer announcements on the day override anything written here.

  1. Build during the event. Anything pre-existing is allowed only if organizers say so, and must be declared.
  2. Open-source libraries, frameworks and public APIs are fine. Respect their licences.
  3. AI tools are allowed. You must be able to explain every line you submit.
  4. Your project must answer your assigned domain and problem. Nothing else scores.
  5. No copying another team’s work, and no submitting work that is not yours.
  6. Teams are locked once registration closes. One person cannot sit in two teams.
  7. Pick a team leader. All official communication goes through them.
  8. Carry a valid college ID. It is checked at the door.
  9. Present within your allotted time. Miss the final presentation and you are out.
  10. Judges’ decisions are final.
  11. Harassment, discrimination or disruption gets you removed. No second warning.
  12. You keep ownership of your work. Organizers may showcase it for event purposes.

Questions

Before you ask a coordinator

Can students from other colleges take part?

Yes. Any college, any department, any year of study. You do not have to be from Jeppiaar.

Do I need a team before I register?

Yes. You register as a team of 2 or 3, with every member’s details entered at once. Teams cannot be changed after registration closes.

What does the ₹299 cover?

Entry for the whole team, not per person, for the full 24 hours.

Do we get the problem statements in advance?

No. The domains are public so you can prepare, but the actual problem statements are revealed at the opening ceremony on 25 September.

What happens if we are not shortlisted?

Round 1 ends with evaluation at 4:30 PM and 15 teams are announced at 5:30 PM. Shortlisted teams continue into the overnight round.

Do we stay the night at the venue?

Yes, selected teams stay overnight at the venue. Accommodation is arranged.

How do we pay?

Direct bank transfer to the official Indian Bank account shown above. Upload the transfer screenshot on the registration page and a coordinator verifies it.

What do we submit at the end?

A working prototype plus a presentation, demonstrated live in front of the judging panel — along with impact metrics and a business case.
